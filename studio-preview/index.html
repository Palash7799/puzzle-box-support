The studio

Small team. Big feelings.

Unrealistic Studio is an independent game studio. We build every game from the first prototype to the last App Store screenshot, and we care about the details: the squish of a jelly block, the crack of a six-shooter, the glow of a hole swallowing a skyscraper.

Every game is free to start, plays in seconds and is designed for short, satisfying sessions. We read every email, and your feedback shapes our updates.
