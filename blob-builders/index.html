Blob Builders

Support

Blob Builders is a physics puzzle game: drag sticky jelly blobs together to build bridges, towers and roads, and lead the survivors safely home.

Contact us Email unrealisticstudio.dev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days. Please include your device model, iOS version and the level number if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play?
Drag a blob from the pile and drop it next to your structure to link it. Triangles make strong shapes; squares wobble. Build across the gap so the blobs can reach the pipe.

I'm stuck. Can I get a hint?
Tap the light bulb button to see a working build as ghost blobs.

How do I zoom or look around?
Pinch to zoom and drag empty space to move the view.

How do I undo?
Tap the undo arrow at the top right.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.

Why are there ads?
Blob Builders is free and supported by ads. Reward ads (hints and Goo Drops) are optional.

Does the app collect my data?
We don't collect personal information. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may use device information to show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
