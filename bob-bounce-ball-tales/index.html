Support

Bob: Bounce Ball Tales is a bouncing-ball platformer: roll, jump and slam Bob, a little red ball, through 8 worlds of gems, traps and secrets.

Contact us Email unrealisticstudio.dev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days. Please include your device model, iOS version and the level name if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play?
Use the left and right buttons to roll, the up button to jump and the down button to slam. Prefer swiping? Tap the blue controls button on the title screen to switch to swipe controls.

How do I open the next world?
Reach the glowing archway at the end of a level. The next level on the world map opens when you finish one.

What do the glowing orbs do?
They change Bob's form for a short time: Rock Bob is heavy and smashes crates, Bubble Bob floats up through water, Speedy Bob races across big gaps.

Can I play in 2D?
Yes. Tap 2D on the title screen to switch to the hand-drawn look.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.

Does the app collect my data?
No. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
