Bob: Bounce Ball Tales

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version

Bob: Bounce Ball Tales does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (which levels are open and your best gem count), your sound setting, your control choice and your 2D/3D choice on your own device. This never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data, we collect no data from children.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or anything else that uses data, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
