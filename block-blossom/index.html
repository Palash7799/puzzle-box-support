Block Blossom

Support

Block Blossom is a relaxing block puzzle: drag pieces into the garden, clear rows and columns, and grow flowers. A new Daily Garden arrives every day.

Contact us Email unrealisticstudio.dev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days. Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play?
Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Pieces with a sprout bloom into flowers when their line clears, and every 4 flowers give you a Bloom Bomb that clears a 3×3 area. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

What is the Daily Garden?
Each day everyone gets the same starting garden and a goal score. Reach the goal to complete the day and grow your streak. Miss a day and the streak starts again.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your best score and streak, and progress does not move between devices.

Why are there ads?
Block Blossom is free and supported by ads. Some ads are optional: watch one to get new pieces or keep playing after the board fills up.

I can't hear any sound.
Check that your iPhone or iPad is not on silent and that the speaker button in the game is on.

Does the app collect my data?
We don't collect personal information. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may use device information to show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
