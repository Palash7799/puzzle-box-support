Bean Rush

Support

Bean Rush is a 3D Wild West crowd-runner: lead your posse of cowboy beans across a canyon bridge, pick the biggest gates, shoot outlaws, dodge saws, bulls and barrels, and win the showdown with the boss.

Contact us Email unrealisticstudio.dev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days. Please include your device model, iOS version and the level number if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play?
Drag left and right to steer your crowd, and swipe up to jump. Pick the lane with the bigger gate (+ and × grow your crowd, − and ÷ shrink it). Your posse shoots automatically at whatever you are lined up with.

What are the three buttons at the bottom?
From level 3 you can tap Dynamite (blows up the nearest obstacle), Poncho (a short shield) and Quick-draw (faster shooting). They recharge after each use.

How do I win a level?
Reach the end of the bridge with a bigger crowd than the boss. Stars depend on how many beans you keep.

Why did my crowd shrink at a gate?
Red gates, the post in the middle of each gate, saws, barrels, logs, outlaws and bulls all cost beans. Dodge them or shoot them down.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.

Why are there ads?
Bean Rush is free and supported by ads. Double Coins and Skip Level are optional video ads, and a short ad may appear between levels.

Does the app collect my data?
We don't collect personal information. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may use device information to show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
