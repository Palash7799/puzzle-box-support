Puzzle Box

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Puzzle Box is a collection of eight puzzle games in one app. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your information when you play.

—We don't collect your name, email, or any account details. There are no accounts.
—Nothing is sent to our own servers. We don't run any.
—Your level progress and stars are stored only on your device.
—The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use a device identifier if you allow it.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Puzzle Box does not ask for your name, email address, or any account details, because it doesn't have accounts.

Each game saves its own progress — which levels you've unlocked, your stars, and your best scores — directly on your device, using standard local app storage. This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Advertising

Puzzle Box is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear between games, and you can choose to watch an optional video ad for a bonus such as a hint or an extra move.

To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone and iPad, the app asks your permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are also shown a consent form, and you can change your choice at any time.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Puzzle Box contains no other analytics software.

Data sharing

We don't sell or share any personal information ourselves. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children's privacy

Puzzle Box is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that does collect information — we'll update this page and change the date at the top. We'll never reduce your privacy without updating this page first.
