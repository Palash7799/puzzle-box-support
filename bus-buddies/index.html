Bus Buddies

Support

Bus Buddies is a colour-matching puzzle: tap passengers to walk them to the bus stop and fill each bus with buddies of its colour.

Contact us Email unrealisticstudio.dev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days. Please include your device model, iOS version and the level number if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play?
Tap a passenger who has a clear path to the top of the park. They walk to the stop and get on the bus if it is their colour. Buses leave when three passengers are on board. Anyone without a bus waits on the bench, and if the bench fills up the level is lost.

Why can't I tap some passengers?
Sleepy-looking passengers are boxed in. Clear a path to them first. Hedges block the way too.

What are the "?" passengers?
Mystery passengers show their colour once they have a clear path out.

I'm stuck on a level.
Every level can be solved with the normal bench. Try a different order, use Undo, or add an extra bench seat.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.

Why are there ads?
Bus Buddies is free and supported by ads. Undo and Extra seat are optional video ads.

Does the app collect my data?
We don't collect personal information. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may use device information to show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
