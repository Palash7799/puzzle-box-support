Bus Buddies

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Bus Buddies is a colour-matching puzzle game by Unrealistic Studio. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your information when you play.

—We don't collect your name, email, or any account details. There are no accounts.
—Your levels, stars and settings are stored only on your device.
—The free game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use a device identifier if you allow it.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Bus Buddies does not ask for your name, email address, or any account details. Your level progress, stars, language and sound settings are saved on your device using standard local app storage and are never sent to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Advertising

Bus Buddies is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear between games, and you can choose to watch an optional video ad for a bonus such as undoing a move or an extra bench seat.

To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone and iPad, the app asks your permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are also shown a consent form, and you can change your choice at any time.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We don't sell or share any personal information ourselves. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Children's privacy

Bus Buddies is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
