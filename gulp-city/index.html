Gulp City

Support

Gulp City is a 3D arcade game: steer a hungry hole through a toy-bright city, swallow everything smaller than you, and be the biggest hole when time runs out.

Contact us Email unrealisticstudio.dev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days. Please include your device model, iOS version and the level number if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play?
Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your hole. Anything smaller than the hole falls in and makes it grow, so start with people and bins, then move up to cars, houses and towers.

Why won't something fall in?
It's bigger than your hole. It will wobble at the edge; come back once you've grown.

What are the other holes?
Rival holes eat the same city. A bigger hole can swallow a smaller one, so steer clear of holes larger than yours and chase the smaller ones.

How do I win a level?
Be the biggest hole when the timer ends. Stars depend on how much of the city you ate.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.

Why are there ads?
Gulp City is free and supported by ads. Start Big and +20 seconds are optional video ads.

Does the app collect my data?
We don't collect personal information. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may use device information to show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.
