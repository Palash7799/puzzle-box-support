Swarm Blaster

Support

Swarm Blaster is a 3D action game: one brave hero, a huge swarm of monsters, and weapons that grow every time you level up. Survive the chapter and beat the boss.

Contact us Email unrealisticstudio.dev@gmail.com. We reply within a few days. Please include your device model, iOS version and the chapter number if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play?
Touch anywhere and drag to move. Your hero fires on his own. Collect the gems monsters drop to level up, then pick one of three upgrades.

What do DASH and BLAST do?
DASH makes you zip forward and slip through a ring of monsters. BLAST charges as you defeat monsters; when it glows, tap it to clear the crowd around you.

How do weapons evolve?
Raise a weapon to its top level and also pick its partner item. The next level-up offers the evolved super weapon.

How do I win a chapter?
Survive until the boss arrives and defeat it. There are 100 chapters across 20 worlds, each with its own monsters and bosses.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.

Why are there ads?
Swarm Blaster is free and supported by ads. Watching a video to revive is always optional.
